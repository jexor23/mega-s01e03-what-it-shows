1 · parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: “Booked for 7 April. The invite says 4 July.” It reaches the desk through the app, email and booking-v2 forms of all tenants. Fix: parse with the tenant’s locale, not a fixed month-first order. Ticket: #1.

The note under the table names the reports the desk read as a one-day move. They stay filed under parser by tenant and are counted in #1. Their dates move back one day instead of swapping (“Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.”), so check them again once the parser fix ships: if they still come in, look at how a date-only value passes through a time zone.

2 · hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at the day-first tenants that run it type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The symptom is the same swap as the parser’s, so the subject lines match and decision 0001 merged them. The cause is the form, not the parser: the parser fix does not touch it. Only reports sent through the booking-v3 form belong here. Fix: print the hint from the tenant’s locale (DD/MM/YYYY for these tenants), as booking-v2 does. Ticket: #2.
