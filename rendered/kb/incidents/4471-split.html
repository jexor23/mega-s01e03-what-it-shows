Ticket 1 · the parser

People see the day and the month swap: a booking for 2 December comes out on 12 February. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. This ticket holds every report the desk files under parser by tenant; 5 of them read as a one-day move and are footnoted to ticket 3. Ticket 1.

Ticket 2 · the booking-v3 date hint

These come only through the booking-v3 form, at day-first tenants. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type the month first as it tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. A parser fix does not close this one: the hint must change. The desk’s table marks this row “no ticket of its own”; now it has one. Ticket 2.

Ticket 3 · the one-day move

Here the date does not swap; it moves by one day. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” One meeting showed Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. That points at a date-only value that goes through a time-zone conversion, not at the parser. The desk read these reports as a one-day move (its notes) and files them under parser by tenant, so they were hidden in that row. Ticket 3.

Since #4471 closed

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October:

  • Ticket 1 is fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Closed.
  • Ticket 2 is won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so its hint no longer reaches anyone. Closed as not planned.
  • Ticket 3 stays open. The update does not mention it, and reading dates day first does not stop a date-only value from moving by one day across time zones. It needs engineering to look at the time-zone handling.
